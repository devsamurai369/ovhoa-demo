Rink Locations

Addresses and directions for the rinks OVHOA officials work across Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky.

Northland Ice Center

10400 Reading Road
Cincinnati, OH 45241

Cincinnati’s first year-round rink, open since 1973. Regulation 200′ × 85′ sheet. Phone: (513) 563-0008

Directions

Queen City Sportsplex

10765 Reading Road
Cincinnati, OH 45241

Two sheets of ice — check your assignment for the rink number. Formerly Sports Plus Arena; renamed in 2024. Phone: (513) 483-0233

Directions

Northern Kentucky Ice Center

2638 Anderson Road
Crescent Springs, KY 41017

The only year-round indoor rink in Northern Kentucky. Two surfaces: a full-size NHL sheet and a smaller studio rink. Phone: (859) 344-1981

Directions

Working a rink that isn’t listed, or spotted something out of date? Let the scheduler know and we’ll add it. Always confirm the rink and start time on Horizon Web Ref before you travel.
